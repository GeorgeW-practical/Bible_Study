This offline Bible Study Verse by Verse series provides a dependable and structured way to study Scripture without requiring a continuous internet connection. Each biblical book is organized into clear, manageable passages, allowing you to move carefully through the text, follow the Biblical author’s unfolding message, and understand individual verses within their proper context.

Because the course is available locally, you can study at your own pace, revisit earlier lessons whenever needed, and remain focused without the distractions of an online platform. Whether used for personal study, teaching preparation, family discipleship, or group discussion, this format offers a lasting and accessible resource for developing a deeper, more consistent understanding of God’s Word.

Updates and Versions

Current version: Version 01.1 · Released September 23, 2026

This offline study is a growing work. New books, chapters, devotionals, and themes are added as they are completed, and existing pages are refined along the way. Each new edition carries its own version number, shown here, at the top of every page, and in the file name.

  • Smaller updates advance the number after the point: Version 01.1, 01.2, and so on.
  • After Version 01.9, the next edition begins a new main number: Version 02.1.

To see whether a newer edition is available, compare the version shown here with the latest version offered where you downloaded this file. Each new edition is a complete replacement: download the new file and open it in your browser.

📖 My Story for making this commentary

This commentary has been years in the making. It began as a labor of love, and it remains one; every study, every verse-by-verse exposition, and every hour spent on it has been offered with the hope that it will help someone understand God's Word more clearly and live it more faithfully.

It has also come at substantial expense. The research tools, the production work, and the time required to do this carefully have not been small, and I have carried that cost gladly because I believe in what this work can do.

My prayer is that this commentary will one day be in the hands of thousands of people: pastors, teachers, small groups, and ordinary believers who simply want to go deeper. That is where you come in.

If this work has been a blessing to you, would you consider making a donation? Any gift, large or small, helps keep this commentary growing and puts it within reach of more people. Thank you, sincerely, for your support and your prayers.
